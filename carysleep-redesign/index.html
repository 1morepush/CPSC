Welcome toCarolina Pulmonary & Sleep Clinic

Welcome to Carolina Pulmonary & Sleep Clinic, the office of Priyan Samarakoon MD. We provide comprehensive clinical care for the full range of Pulmonary, Critical Care and Sleep disorders in both in-patient and out-patient settings.

Carolina Pulmonary & Sleep Clinic is located in Cary, North Carolina. We specialize in pulmonary diseases, Critical Care and Sleep Medicine. We are committed to providing the best care for our patients and promoting lifelong health. Our patients' emotional and physical well-being is our highest priority, and we will always treat our patients with the utmost respect and discretion.

We hope to be clear in our communication with you, and we strive to eliminate inconvenience and improve the quality of life for our patients through promotion of health awareness and proper disease management.
