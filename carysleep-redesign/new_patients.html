New Patients

(A) Please remember to bring the following to your appointment:

  • Insurance ID card (Up-to-date insurance information).
  • Driving license or Military ID
  • List of all the medications
  • Reports of relevant lab test, chest x-rays, CT scans, pulmonary function tests or sleep studies.
  • CD's of all the Chest X rays, CT scan and PET scans with reports
  • Per insurance regulations: All patients are required to pay the Co-pay, deductible and Co-Insurance.
  • Directions to office - Click for the office in Cary, NC or office in Fayetteville, NC

(B) New Patients

  • Call our office to get you access to the patient portal or download the Patient Forms.
  • Click the Patient Portal and complete the intake forms online.
  • Log in and scroll on to the left column.

      • (a) Go to "My Account" on to the left column

    • Click "Personal Information" and " Additional Information".
    • Enter demographic data: date of birth,address, phone number, Insurance information, pharmacy etc.

      (b) Go to "Questionnaire" and click "Medical History" on the left column

    • All patients complete " Past Medical History"
    • Complete the "Sleep Medicine Questionnaire" if you have a sleep issues.
    • Complete the "Pulmonary Medicine Questionnaire" if you have respiratory symptoms.

(C) Established Patients

  • “Bill Pay” securely via the Patient Portal
  • Check and update information, change / cancel appointments
  • Send and obtain e messages via the patient portal
  • Receive appointment reminders via e mail
